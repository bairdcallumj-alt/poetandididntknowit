Devices to watch for

Persona
A voice or character adopted by a writer or performer.
Direct address
Speaking directly to the audience.
Allusion
A reference to another event, person, text or idea.
Repetition
Repeating words, phrases, sounds or ideas for effect.
Irony
A contrast between what is said or expected and the deeper reality.
Juxtaposition
Placing contrasting ideas close together so their differences stand out.
Paradox
An apparently contradictory idea that may reveal a deeper truth.
Imagery
Language that creates a strong sensory or mental picture.
